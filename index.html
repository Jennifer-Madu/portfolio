About Me

Making Data Work for You.

I am a data analyst with an engineering background and a strong interest in transforming raw data into structured, meaningful and actionable information.

My work focuses on data cleaning, wrangling, transformation and exploratory data analysis. I use Python, Pandas and Excel to organise datasets, identify inconsistencies, uncover patterns and prepare data for informed decision-making.

I enjoy working with complex datasets and turning disorganised information into reliable analytical outputs that individuals and businesses can use.
